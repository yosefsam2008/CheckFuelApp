Two ways to redesign the app, shown on the two screens that matter: the home screen and the result. Hebrew, right-to-left, sample data. Switch the preview between each direction's native look, light, and dark.

All colours in the specs pass WCAG AA (measured). Sample numbers are illustrative.
Recommendation

Go with A, Plate.

  • It is built to be shared. The result is a yellow plate with one big number. A screenshot of it in a WhatsApp group chat is the app's advertising, and no other element on the screen needs to be more recognisable.
  • It fits the audience. Teen and young drivers, mostly one phone, usually in the evening or in the car: dark-first, big thumb targets, one loud colour, zero reading.
  • It is cheap to build well. One accent, two weights of one font family, tone-step depth. Fewer moving parts than B, and it replaces the five competing palettes with one.
  • B is the safer second. If you want a calmer, broader, parent-friendly face, B is the one. Say so and I will build B instead; nothing below depends on the choice.

Shared by both: structure

  • Navigation: a native bottom tab bar with three tabs: Calculate, Trips, Vehicles. Three destinations, thumb reach, always visible, back swipe works, and it is what iOS and Android users expect. The hamburger menu goes. About, Legal and Guide move behind an info icon in the top bar.
  • One-screen calculator: distance, vehicle, price and passengers on one screen, with a single "Calculate" button, instead of a five-step wizard. The last vehicle is preselected.
  • Ads off the happy path: one small banner below the result, never between the question and the answer, and the full-screen ad only after "new calculation".
  • Cut or merged: dashboard (already removed), the guide as a standalone screen, the second add-vehicle screen, the trip-details modal (expand in the list instead).
A · Recommended

Plate

Loud, quick, shareable. Takes its look from the thing every Israeli driver reads daily: the licence plate. Dark by default, one yellow, huge numerals.

Mood and marketing

Fast, a bit cheeky, trustworthy where it counts (the number). "Split it with friends" is the hook: the result is already formatted as a picture you would post.

The blue strip nods at a plate; it is not a replica of any real plate.

Palette (dark / light)

Measured: ink on ground 17.0:1 (dark), 17.1:1 (light); secondary text 7.8:1 / 6.4:1; text on yellow 13.2:1 / 12.5:1; borders 3.7:1 (dark), 3.2:1 (light).

Type

Heebo only, with Hebrew and Latin drawn together. Weight 900 for the plate numeral, 800 for headings, 500–700 for UI. Tabular figures on every amount. Respects the system text size.

Space, shape, depth

  • Spacing on a 4-point scale: 4 8 12 16 24 32
  • Radius 10 / 16 / 22
  • Depth from tone steps. The only shadow is a hard offset under the main button.
  • Targets 44 pt and up; the main button is 56.

Motion

"Snap": 120–200 ms ease-out. Press compresses the button's offset shadow. The cost counts up once (about 600 ms), then a light haptic. With Reduce Motion: a plain cross-fade, no count-up.

Trade-offs

  • Yellow is only ever a fill, never text on light.
  • Strong identity: harder to soften later.
  • Teen-leaning; parents may find it loud.
B · Alternative

Open Road

Calm, clear, trustworthy. Takes its look from intercity road signs: deep sign green with a white inner border, a warm amber "go" button, light by default.

Mood and marketing

Clear, steady, helpful. Reads as a tool you can trust with money, so it travels better to parents and older drivers. Less thumb-stopping on a social feed.

Palette (light / dark)

groundsurfacesigngobad
groundsurfacesigngobad

Measured: ink on ground 14.1:1 / 16.4:1; secondary text 6.1:1 / 7.6:1; white on sign green 6.5:1; ink on amber 8.8:1; borders 3.3:1 (light), 4.2:1 (dark).

Type

Rubik for headings and numerals (rounded, friendly, full Hebrew), Assistant for body and UI. Weights 400–600. Tabular figures on amounts.

Space, shape, depth

  • 4-point spacing scale, same as A
  • Radius 12 / 20 / 28
  • Depth from 1 px borders and tone steps; no shadows
  • Targets 44 pt and up; main button 56

Motion

"Glide": 220–320 ms, gentle springs. The sign card slides in, the number rolls once. Light haptic on success. With Reduce Motion: cross-fade only.

Trade-offs

  • Green plus amber sits close to many eco and finance apps.
  • Less distinctive on its own, relies on the sign-card detail.
  • Very safe; hard to get wrong.
